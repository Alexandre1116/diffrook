Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Responsibility and contact

Alexandre Ramos, Diogo Mariano and Guilherme Pereira, acting individually in Portugal, are jointly responsible for the purposes and means of processing personal data for diffrook.dev, direct enquiries and licence administration. Contact all three, or exercise your rights, through hello@diffrook.dev. You may address a request to any of them through this shared contact.

For data processed inside a self-hosted installation, the organisation or person operating that installation decides the purposes and configuration. Contact that operator for its own privacy notice and requests concerning instance data. Installing Diffrook does not itself give the maintainers access to the instance database or repository contents.

2. Website visits and enquiries

The website is delivered through Cloudflare. Network and security processing can include IP address, requested URL, time, browser information and security events to deliver pages and protect the service. We rely on our legitimate interests in operating and securing the website, subject to your rights and interests.

If you email us, we receive your email address, name if supplied, message and attachments. We use them to answer your request, provide support and keep necessary correspondence. The basis is taking steps at your request before a contract or performing a contract where applicable; otherwise our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Supply only information necessary for your request. We do not use support messages for advertising.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not include analytics or advertising scripts or set analytics or advertising cookies in its application code. Cloudflare may process technical information and use necessary security mechanisms when protecting the site. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising profiles.

The self-hosted app uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. Its default lifetime is eight hours; the administrator can configure between five minutes and 24 hours. OpenID Connect sign-in also uses a temporary security cookie. These support authentication rather than advertising.

When licence purchasing is available, the licence page can save a private download token under diffrook-last-licence in local storage to reopen your licence. Clearing this website’s storage removes the saved browser copy. Treat licence links and tokens as confidential. Stripe may apply its own necessary storage during payment or billing portal use.

4. Licences and payments

New paid purchases are currently disabled, and Diffrook Cloud is not available. This section describes existing licence administration and the implemented checkout when enabled; it does not mean a Cloud service is currently processing customer repositories.

Licence records can contain the holder’s name, installation identifier, plan, allowances, billing period, amounts, payment and subscription references, signed licence and expiry. Stripe handles payment details; our commerce database does not store full card numbers or card security codes. Order and licence records are stored using Cloudflare D1. Download tokens are stored as hashes on the server. We process these records to perform the licence contract, meet applicable accounting and tax obligations, and prevent fraud or defend legal claims where necessary.

5. Data within a self-hosted instance

The instance stores account names, password hashes, session records, configuration, integration credentials, automation settings and run history in its data directory and database. With SSO, records can also include the identity provider’s issuer, subject and email information. Repository context, prompts, model responses and action results can contain personal data.

Enabled integrations send the data needed for the selected action to configured code hosts, AI providers, notification destinations and identity providers. For example, a review may send code, diffs, issue text and comments to the selected AI provider, and publish the response on the code host. The server also contacts GitHub to check for releases, exposing ordinary connection metadata to GitHub. These flows depend on the operator’s configuration; self-hosting does not mean all data stays on that server.

The operator must determine its legal basis, permissions, retention, access controls and any agreements or transfer safeguards needed for these services. The maintainers receive instance content only if it is separately provided to them, for example in a support request. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal data in such requests.

6. Recipients and international processing

Data is accessible to the maintainers as needed for the purposes above, to providers supporting hosting, security, correspondence and applicable payments, and to authorities where legally required. Cloudflare provides website delivery and commerce storage; Stripe provides payment and billing services when used. GitHub receives data when you follow repository links or use its services.

Providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where we are responsible for a restricted transfer, applicable safeguards must be in place, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy, Stripe’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. Retention and security

We retain enquiries for the time needed to resolve them and any related support or dispute. Licence and transaction records are retained for licence administration and applicable accounting, tax and legal claim periods. Technical records are retained as needed for delivery, troubleshooting and security, subject to the relevant provider settings. We do not promise a fixed deletion deadline where a legal obligation requires retention; contact us for the period applicable to your record.

Self-hosted retention and backups are controlled by the operator. The application does not impose a general automatic expiry on all configuration and run history. Deleting a browser cookie does not delete server records or backups. Operators must manage deletion and backup retention separately.

We use access restrictions and security mechanisms appropriate to the service. No system is completely secure. Keep private download links and credentials confidential and report concerns to our contact address.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. These rights are subject to their legal conditions; we may need proportionate information to verify your identity.

Email hello@diffrook.dev for data we control. For self-hosted data, contact your instance operator. You may complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) or another competent supervisory authority. We do not use website or licence records for automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Changes

We will update this notice when practices change and show the revision date. Any future Cloud service, optional analytics or materially different processing will require updated information before it is introduced.
