The Individual plan has no license fee for permitted use, with no expiry or

The paid self-hosted subscription plans are:

- Teams: up to five registered users and twenty saved automations, including

- Enterprise: purchased registered users, with a minimum of ten, and ten saved

  SSO. Additional packs of ten automations may be purchased.
